## exact-calculation limitations

- 3-bucket joint global/train/test is NOT computed (C-subsample
  dependence across blocks needs full composition enumeration).
  Reported: exact marginals at NTR/T plus Frechet bounds
  [max(0,PG+PTR+... ), min(...)] in review text, not as estimates.
- R-b (within-block re-application of C conditions) joint likewise
  not computed; marginals at NTR/T are exact fresh-sample values.
- Double-precision log-space; terms < ~5e-324 recorded as 0-dagger
  (underflow), distinct from 0* structural zeros.
