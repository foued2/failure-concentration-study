## C9 — Bank generation pinned to byte-reproducibility (BANK_DESIGN §4 :62–79) — REVISED in v1.2.2-draft

**v1.0 text (:62–66):**
> `- Generator: Python 3.11+ random.Random (to be pinned exactly by the execution protocol before any solver execution; bank regeneration is deterministic under the pinned interpreter).`
> `- Seeds: SEED_HELDOUT = 20261101, SEED_DIAGNOSTIC = 11052026. Seeded generators are created independently per bank; record order follows generation order.`
**v1.0 text (:67–69):**
> `- Every record carries: input_id, amount, coins (as-given order), canonical_id, stratum, rule_id, rule_params, and a record_sha256 computed over the canonical JSON serialization of the record at file freeze.`

**Defect carried forward.** Seeds, ranges and guards did not fully determine the sampling process. v1.2.1 pinned most per-stratum construction rules but left a cross-bank exemplar collision, a false D4 parity guarantee, an incomplete repeated-use failsafe, a D4 redraw contradiction, and several implementation obligations insufficiently operationalized.

**Findings from the C9 dry run.** Claude's dry run found that the frozen D5 exemplar (amount 6, coins {1,3,4}) duplicates held-out H8 record 7; D4 records 3–4 do not guarantee answer zero; and H4 had no record satisfying the repeated-use predicate. Claude subsequently withdrew two false gaps in its report (D3 gcd draw order and H2 denomination-count range) and corrected the D4 draw-order description. Claude also reported that the proposed D5 replacement below did not collide with its realized held-out bank. Because the scratch bank was deleted and was not committed, GPT has not independently reproduced that particular realized-bank check. The complete item-8 canonical-disjointness check remains mandatory and controls acceptance.

**Additional implementation defects identified in review.** The committed dry-run harness contains internal unbounded retry loops that bypass the outer 1,000-attempt cap; D4's gcd redraw restarts only the t-set despite the general whole-record rule; its permutation predicate does not reject identity permutations; it accepts a command-line output-directory argument despite item 6; and it writes files before checking cross-bank disjointness. The harness is a diagnostic review artifact, not a conforming production generator. These defects are addressed by the operational rules below.

**Replacement text (exact; supersedes the C9 replacement in v1.2.1-draft):**

> **Sampling and construction conventions (pinned before any bank file exists):**
>
> **1. Uniform inclusive ranges.** Every stated inclusive interval [lo, hi] is sampled uniformly. A general draw is made with `rng.randrange(lo, hi + 1)`. A quantity fixed by construction consumes no random draw. No call uses a degenerate one-value range.
>
> **2. Per-stratum draw order.** The JSON key order is independent of random draw order. Each row below gives the complete draw order. A value derived from another field is computed only after its source field has been drawn. Forced placements consume no random draw. No unlisted field draw or random source is permitted. Where a row says to draw further denominations from a stated interval "excluding" values already placed in the same coin array, implement that exclusion using the coin-slot redraw rule in item 3: draw each slot from the full stated interval with `randrange`, and if its value duplicates any denomination already placed, redraw only that slot. Do not narrow the interval or pre-filter a candidate list. This rejection-sampling rule gives a uniform draw over the then-available denominations while preserving the specified slot-only redraw semantics.
>
> **3. Candidate-attempt and retry protocol.**
>
> A whole-record candidate attempt begins at the first random draw in that stratum's row and continues through all fields, derived values, and required checks. A whole-record rejection discards the entire candidate and restarts that record from its first draw. The only exception is a duplicate denomination encountered while filling a coin-array slot: redraw that slot immediately, without redrawing earlier fields. Each coin-array slot is limited to 1,000 randrange attempts within a single whole-record or whole-pair candidate attempt. This counter resets when that candidate is discarded and restarted from its first draw; it is not cumulative across distinct candidate attempts for the same record or pair slot. Exhaustion aborts generation as a generator defect.
>
> Other whole-candidate rejection conditions include: a min-coin construction with minimum denomination 1; a gcd or parity condition that is not met; a D5 greedy/minimum-count equality; an H2/H4/H8 threshold or answer-mandate failure; an int32 overflow; a within-bank canonical collision; or, during diagnostic generation, a collision with the frozen held-out identity set H. This sentence summarizes the whole-candidate rejection path; it is not the complete or closed reason-code vocabulary. The exact one-code-per-rejection mapping is defined by the common candidate pipeline and item 5. A held-out candidate that unexpectedly matches a fixed D5 key in F_D remains an immediate fatal abort under item 3d, not a retry. Separately, a D6/H6 non-identity permutation rejects the whole pair instance with IDENTITY_PERMUTATION; it is a pair-instance rejection, not a single-record rejection. There are no inner while-until-success loops. All whole-record rejection reasons are logged. Each record slot is limited to 1,000 whole-record candidate attempts in total over its generation. The counter is cumulative for that slot and is not reset between retries; exhaustion aborts generation as a generator defect and is not converted into a study result.
>
> A D6 or H6 reorder-pair candidate is one whole instance, including amount, n, coins, and shuffle. It is rejected and redrawn from its first draw if it fails validity, int32, within-bank canonical-collision, or non-identity-permutation checks. Each pair slot is limited to 1,000 whole-instance candidate attempts in total over its generation. The counter is cumulative for that pair slot and is not reset between retries. A pair is accepted only if its two coin arrays differ in order, while their sorted denominations and amount agree exactly. The original and permutation records are adjacent and share one canonical_id and one pair_id.
>
> The common candidate pipeline is: (a) validate amount, n, denomination bounds and within-record distinctness; any violation aborts as a generator defect; (b) compute the LC518 combinations-count oracle once per canonical instance (for a permutation pair, compute once and reuse the answer for both records); if the answer exceeds 2,147,483,647, reject the whole record or pair instance with `INT32_OVERFLOW`; (c) apply every stratum-specific oracle mandate in item 3a below. The H1 amount-zero answer-1 condition is an assertion, not a candidate rejection: if it fails, abort as a generator defect. Every other rejectable stratum mandate must map to exactly one reason code enumerated in item 5; a failed mandate with no enumerated reason aborts as a generator defect rather than creating an undeclared sidecar reason; (d) check within-bank canonical identity against that bank's reserved and accepted identities; a collision rejects the whole random record or pair instance with `WITHIN_BANK_CANONICAL_COLLISION`, except that the two records of the same declared permutation pair share one canonical identity by design; (e) during held-out generation, compare each random candidate with the diagnostic fixed-key set `F_D` and abort on a hit as specified in item 3d; during diagnostic generation, compare each random candidate or pair identity with the frozen held-out set `H` and reject a hit with `CROSS_BANK_COLLISION`; (f) after both banks have been assembled in memory, run the complete cross-bank canonical-disjointness check in item 8 BEFORE writing any authoritative bank file. A failure at item 8 aborts acceptance and writes no authoritative bank or oracle-output files.
>

The strata run in order D1 through D8 for the diagnostic bank and H1 through H8 for the held-out bank. Within each stratum, records run in increasing k. A paired instance emits its original record followed immediately by its permutation.

> **3b. Repeated-use mandate (exact predicate and fixed witnesses).** For an amount a and distinct denomination set C, define N0(a,C) as the number of subsets U of C for which the sum of denominations in U equals a. Because denominations are distinct, this is the number of combinations that use each denomination at most once. The repeated-use predicate is exactly oracle_answer(a,C) > N0(a,C). The generator computes N0 by a 0/1 subset-count dynamic program over the distinct denominations; it must not infer the predicate from an example heuristic.
>
> The required witness is fixed in advance in each of H1–H4: H1 record 4, H2 record 5, H3 record 1, H4 record 3. The exact records and their oracle/N0 values are given in item 3a. These witnesses are not generated by a post-generation replacement loop and consume no RNG beyond any explicitly stated draw (all four are fixed records). The generator must verify the predicate independently for each witness and abort if any fails. It must also log the predicate result for every record in H1–H4. No random search for a witness is permitted; this removes the previously unspecified stratum-regeneration rule.
>
> **3c. Fixed-record canonical-key reservation (before either RNG stream is consumed).** Before the first random candidate is drawn for either bank, enumerate every fully fixed record and compute its canonical identity as `(amount, tuple(sorted(coins)))`: D5 records 1–2; H1 record 4; H2 record 5; H3 record 1; H4 record 3; and H8 record 7. Verify that these fixed identities are pairwise distinct within each bank and that the fixed-identity sets are disjoint across banks. Any violation aborts before the first random draw as a specification defect. Initialize each bank's reserved canonical-key set with all fixed identities for that bank. Every random single-record or pair candidate is rejected as a whole record/instance with reason `WITHIN_BANK_CANONICAL_COLLISION` if its canonical identity is already reserved or already accepted in that same bank; the candidate restarts from its first draw under item 3. At its prescribed generation position, each fixed record is emitted without an RNG draw and is not tested as a random candidate against its own reservation; its key remains in the reserved/accepted union for the rest of generation. The reservation protects fixed records even when their rows occur after earlier random rows. In particular, H8 record 7's key `(amount 6, sorted coins [1,3,4])` is reserved before H1 records 5–12 are sampled, so a matching H1 candidate is rejected as a whole H1 record and the fixed H8 record is never discarded. D5 records 3–8 can themselves draw either D5 fixed exemplar; because D5 records 1–2 are reserved before D5 record 3 is sampled, such a duplicate candidate is rejected as a whole D5 record. This is a collision rejection, not a claim that the D5 random ranges exclude those keys. For a D6/H6 permutation pair, its one shared canonical key is accepted once and the two members of that same declared pair remain the only permitted within-bank duplicate records. Cross-bank candidate checks and their distinct rejection/abort behavior are specified in item 3d; item 8 remains the final independent verification gate.
>
>
> **3d. Held-out-first generation and cross-bank collision handling (owner-selected policy).** The bank priority is fixed: the held-out bank is generated first and is not filtered against any random diagnostic candidate. The diagnostic bank is generated only after the complete held-out canonical-identity set has been frozen in memory. This makes the diagnostic bank's accepted contents conditional on the held-out bank, by design.
>
> **Preflight before either RNG stream is consumed.** The generator must compute the fixed-key sets from item 3c: \(F_D\) for D5 records 1–2 and \(F_H\) for H1 record 4, H2 record 5, H3 record 1, H4 record 3 and H8 record 7. It must mechanically confirm that each set is internally unique and that \(F_D \cap F_H = \varnothing\); any failed fixed-key check aborts before either RNG stream is consumed as a specification defect. The following structural-exclusion argument is a design-time proof, not a runtime calculation the generator is expected to perform. A reviewer must verify it against the frozen stratum rules before generation and repeat that review whenever a relevant stratum rule, range or fixed exemplar changes: neither fixed D5 exemplar can occur in any held-out stratum because H1 excludes them by denomination count (H1 has at most 4 denominations; each exemplar has 5); H2 excludes them by amount (H2 amounts are at least 13; exemplar amounts are 8 and 9); H3 excludes them by denomination count (H3 has exactly 1 denomination); H4 excludes them by denomination count (H4 has exactly 2 denominations); H5 excludes them by denomination count (H5 has at least 30 denominations); H6 excludes them by amount (H6 amounts are at least 3,500); H7 excludes them because H7 requires oracle answer exactly 0 while both exemplars have positive answers; and H8 excludes them because every H8 record contains anchor {1,2,3} or {1,3,4}, while neither exemplar contains either anchor. If this proof cannot be verified, generation must not be authorized until the specification is corrected and reviewed. The executable runtime backstop is the held-out per-candidate comparison against \(F_D\) in the next section: any hit is logged as `CROSS_BANK_COLLISION` and aborts immediately. Keep the separate per-bank fixed-key reservation sets from item 3c.
>
> **Held-out generation.** Instantiate only `rng_hold = random.Random(20261101)` and generate H1 through H8, records in increasing stratum/record order, using the exact field-draw order in item 3a. A random held-out candidate is checked against \(F_H\) and the already accepted held-out key set. A within-held-out collision rejects the whole record or pair instance with `WITHIN_BANK_CANONICAL_COLLISION` and restarts it from its first draw. A held-out candidate that matches any key in \(F_D\) contradicts the structural-exclusion check above: record `CROSS_BANK_COLLISION` for that candidate and abort generation immediately as a specification/generator defect; do not silently retry it. Fixed held-out records are emitted at their prescribed positions without RNG draws. When all 80 records are complete and all held-out checks pass, freeze the complete set \(H\) of their canonical identities in memory. Do not write any bank, oracle or report file at this stage.
>
> **Diagnostic generation.** Only after \(H\) is frozen, instantiate `rng_diag = random.Random(11052026)` and generate D1 through D8 in the exact field-draw order in item 3a. For each random diagnostic single-record candidate or reorder-pair instance, apply the ordinary validity, oracle, stratum and within-diagnostic checks. If its canonical identity is already in the diagnostic bank's own reserved/accepted set, reject the whole candidate with `WITHIN_BANK_CANONICAL_COLLISION`. Otherwise, if its canonical identity is in \(H\), reject the whole candidate or pair instance with `CROSS_BANK_COLLISION`; discard all fields from that attempt and restart that diagnostic record/pair from its first draw. Do not consume, rewind or otherwise modify `rng_hold` during diagnostic generation. Each rejected cross-bank attempt consumes exactly the diagnostic-stream draws already made by that attempt. Fixed D5 records are emitted at their prescribed positions without RNG draws; the preflight structural-exclusion check guarantees they cannot collide with an admissible held-out input, and item 8 verifies that claim against the realized held-out bank. For D6, compare the pair's one canonical identity against \(H\) once; if it collides, reject and redraw the whole pair instance. The two orderings within one accepted D6 pair remain the only allowed duplicate canonical records inside the diagnostic bank.
>
> **Counts, limits and logs.** Rejected candidates do not consume a stratum's accepted-record quota. Continue each stratum until its exact specified count is accepted, subject to the existing 1,000-attempt limit per whole record or whole pair instance and 1,000-attempt limit per coin-array slot. Each `CROSS_BANK_COLLISION` and `WITHIN_BANK_CANONICAL_COLLISION` is one whole-candidate rejection event in the appropriate bank's sidecar, with the one-based attempt number, bank, candidate_id and stratum. A held-out-versus-F_D structural contradiction is logged as `CROSS_BANK_COLLISION` and then aborts immediately; it is not retried. Exhausting any attempt limit aborts the whole generation as a generator defect. The original 64/80 record totals, stratum counts, seeds, fixed witnesses, field ranges and per-stratum draw orders do not change. The realized number of `CROSS_BANK_COLLISION` rejections is not known before generation and must not be replaced by a probability estimate; item 8 requires the exact realized counts for all 16 strata.
>
> **Statistical interpretation and exact replacement for BANK_DESIGN §7 item 2.** The two PRNG objects remain separate, with their states never shared. However, the accepted bank contents are not statistically independent samples: the held-out bank is generated from its own frozen rules first, and accepted diagnostic random records are selected conditional on avoiding \(H\). The held-out bank is not conditioned on diagnostic random records or solver outcomes; its only cross-bank preflight constraint is the proof that the fixed D5 records cannot occur in any held-out stratum. The study must not describe the two accepted banks as independent samples. Replace BANK_DESIGN.md §7 item 2 with: "The held-out bank is generated first using its dedicated seeded stream. The diagnostic bank is generated second using its separate seeded stream, and every random diagnostic candidate is rejected if its canonical identity occurs in the frozen held-out bank. This ensures canonical disjointness by construction while making the accepted diagnostic bank conditional on the held-out bank. The two random-number streams are separate; the accepted bank contents are not statistically independent samples. The held-out bank is not filtered against random diagnostic candidates or solver outcomes." This replacement supersedes the earlier §7.2 wording that calls the seeded generators independent and says the strata draw from disjoint parameter spaces. The frozen source file remains byte-unchanged until an owner-approved design acceptance applies this replacement.

> | D6 | H1, H2, H7, H8 | n=3–8 and amount 10–200; overlap exists for small H1/H2 inputs, unreachable H7 inputs, and anchored H8 inputs. |
> | D7 | H5 | Only H5 reaches n=50–120; common amounts are 100–500. |
> | D8 | H5, H6, H7 | H5 overlap is at amount 2,000 and n=30; H6 overlaps at n=5–30 and amounts 3,500–5,000; unreachable D8 inputs can overlap H7. |
>
> **Structural explanations for selected table absences (pairwise reasoning, not exhaustive enumeration).** The following four absent pairs are impossible under the pinned stratum rules:
> - **D3–H2:** D3 candidates have oracle answer exactly 0; every H2 record is required to have oracle answer at least 2.
> - **D1–H8:** if a D1 candidate contains coin 1, then c_min = 1 and its largest prescribed amount is 2·c_min + 1 = 3; H8 requires amount at least 6. If D1 does not contain coin 1, it cannot equal either H8 anchor.
> - **D4–H8:** every D4 denomination set is all-odd or all-even, whereas each H8 anchor ({1,2,3} or {1,3,4}) contains both odd and even denominations.
> - **D2–H7:** a D2 amount is itself one of its denominations. Therefore the minimum denomination cannot exceed the amount, excluding H7's min-coin rule; and the gcd of all denominations divides that denomination (the amount), excluding H7's non-divisible-gcd rule.
>
> These are direct pairwise deductions from the stated rules. They are not the result of exhaustive enumeration of canonical keys.
>
> A listed overlap means some keys are possible, not that all candidates in that diagnostic stratum are removed. For the realized run, the exact excluded set in D_i is D_i's admissible candidate support intersected with H. The actual number of rejected whole candidates is recorded by stratum as required below. In particular, D5 random records 3–8 can produce the fixed H8 key (amount 6, coins {1,3,4}): n=3, further coins {3,4}, amount=6. Under the pinned uniform draws, the probability for this specific key on one candidate attempt is (1/3) × (1/28) × (1/26) = 1/2,184 ≈ 0.000458, not 0.0014. This is an illustrative single-key probability only; it is not the realized D5 rejection rate or the total D5/H8 overlap probability.
>
> **Directionality of held-out collision counts (structural, not empirical).** The held-out bank is generated before random diagnostic candidates exist, so held-out candidates are never filtered against random diagnostic records. The only held-out-side cross-bank check is against the fixed D5 key set F_D. Any hit is logged as a fatal contradiction and aborts the run; it is not an ordinary retry and cannot produce a completed PASS run. Retryable cross-bank collision rejections therefore occur only during diagnostic generation. Consequently, H1–H8 counts in a completed PASS report must all be zero. This is a logical consequence of the protocol, not an observed zero collision rate.
>
> **Realized collision counts.** Every output report must include the exact number of CROSS_BANK_COLLISION whole-candidate rejection events for each of D1–D8 and H1–H8, including explicit zero values. Counts are taken from the rejection sidecars, not estimated from the support map. The report counts must reconcile exactly with sidecar rows. Under the held-out-first protocol, all H1–H8 counts must be zero for a completed run; a held-out/F_D hit is a logged fatal preflight contradiction and makes the run NOT_ACCEPTED. The D1–D8 counts quantify the realized support exclusions for this seeded run; they must be reported by stratum and not described as measured probabilities.
>
> **Exact rule_params values.** In H1, edge=true only for records 1–3 and repeated_use_witness=true only for record 4. In H2, forced_coin_1=true only for records 1–4, match_amount=true only for record 1, and repeated_use_witness=true only for record 5. In H3, repeated_use_witness=true only for record 1. In H4, match_amount=true only for records 1–2 and repeated_use_witness=true only for record 3. In H6, singleton=true only for records 1–6. In D5, the exemplar_id is D5-E1 for record 1 and D5-E2 for record 2; coin_1_fixed=true only for records 3–8. D4 mechanism values are exactly those stated in its row. H7 mechanism values are exactly min-coin for records 1–4 and gcd for records 5–8. In H8, anchor is 1-2-3 for records 1–6 and 1-3-4 for records 7–12; anchor_fixed=true for all H8 records. A Boolean key is present on every record in its declared stratum schema and takes the value specified here; keys not applicable to a record are omitted only where the row explicitly defines an alternative schema.
>
> > **4. RNG protocol.** Use exactly two separate streams: `rng_hold = random.Random(20261101)` and `rng_diag = random.Random(11052026)`. Generate the held-out bank first using `rng_hold` only; after the accepted held-out canonical set H is frozen, instantiate and use `rng_diag` for the diagnostic bank. No random state is shared between streams. Within each bank, consume its stream in the stratum order and field order pinned in item 3a. A rejected whole-record or whole-instance candidate consumes all draws made by that attempt from that bank's stream; the next attempt starts at the first draw of that same candidate construction. A duplicate coin-slot draw consumes only the draw calls for that slot. A diagnostic `CROSS_BANK_COLLISION` consumes only the diagnostic-stream draws already made by that rejected attempt and never changes the held-out stream. Use only `random.Random.randrange` and `random.Random.shuffle` (Fisher–Yates for D6/H6). No random module globals, `sample`, numpy, time-based seed, entropy source, external configuration, or additional RNG is permitted. Separate PRNG streams do not make the accepted bank contents statistically independent; the diagnostic bank is selected conditional on the frozen held-out set as specified in item 3d.
>
> **5. Identifiers, canonical identity, serialization and record hashes.**
>
> - input_id is the bank prefix followed by a three-digit, one-based global record ordinal in that bank: D001–D064 or H001–H080. Pair records receive consecutive ordinals in original-then-permutation order.
> - rule_id is exactly the stratum identifier: D1–D8 or H1–H8. No other spelling or suffix is permitted.
- stratum is exactly the same D1–D8 or H1–H8 string as rule_id; the two fields must agree.
> - For D6/H6 pairs, pair_id is D6-P01 through D6-P04 or H6-P01 through H6-P02. ordering is exactly original or permutation. Both records carry the same pair_id and canonical_id.
> - canonical identity is (amount, tuple(sorted(coins))). canonical_id is C- followed by the lowercase SHA-256 hex digest of the UTF-8 bytes of the canonical JSON payload {"amount": amount, "coins": sorted_coins}, serialized by json.dumps with sort_keys=True, separators=(",",":"), ensure_ascii=False and allow_nan=False. This identity is order-invariant and identical across banks for the same mathematical input.
> - rule_params contains only the stratum-specific fields listed in item 3a; all keys and allowed value types are fixed by the row. Empty rule_params is {}. No undeclared key is permitted.
> - The oracle-output attachments are exactly `diagnostic_oracle_outputs.json` and `heldout_oracle_outputs.json`, one per bank. Each is one UTF-8 JSON array with one object per distinct canonical_id in that bank, ordered by the first appearance of that canonical_id in bank generation order. Each object has exactly the fields `{canonical_id, amount, coins, oracle_answer}`; `coins` is sorted ascending and `oracle_answer` is the LC518 unbounded-combination count for that canonical instance. The two records of a D6/H6 permutation pair therefore share one oracle-output object. Serialize each array as `json.dumps(rows, sort_keys=True, separators=(",",":"), ensure_ascii=False, allow_nan=False).encode("utf-8") + b"\n"`. The file-level SHA-256 is the lowercase hexadecimal digest of those exact bytes. Every record in the corresponding bank carries `oracle_output_file_sha256`, set to that bank attachment's file-level digest; `canonical_id` identifies its row in the attachment. This field is computed before record hashes and is part of the record-hash payload below.
> - The record-hash payload contains exactly input_id, amount, coins (as-given order), canonical_id, stratum, rule_id, rule_params, int32_guard and oracle_output_file_sha256. int32_guard is {status: "accepted" or "redrawn", redraws: k}; status is redrawn only if at least one earlier whole candidate for that accepted record was rejected by the int32 guard; redraws counts only int32-overflow rejections. Other rejection reasons and their attempt numbers are recorded in the sidecar log. For D6/H6 permutation pairs, the int32 guard is evaluated once for the shared canonical instance, and both records carry identical `int32_guard` objects reflecting that shared pair-instance attempt history; the redraw count is not counted separately per record. record_sha256 is excluded from its own payload. Serialize the payload with json.dumps(payload, sort_keys=True, separators=(",",":"), ensure_ascii=False, allow_nan=False) and UTF-8 encoding. Integers use JSON decimal notation; no floats or NaN values are allowed. record_sha256 is the lowercase hexadecimal SHA-256 digest of those payload bytes and is added only to the written record.
> - The authoritative bank filenames remain exactly diagnostic_bank.json and heldout_bank.json as named in BANK_DESIGN §9. Each file is one UTF-8 JSON array of record objects in generation order, with no BOM. Each record object is the complete payload plus record_sha256. Serialize the entire array with json.dumps(records, sort_keys=True, separators=(",",":"), ensure_ascii=False, allow_nan=False), encode as UTF-8, and append one LF byte. This preserves a valid JSON file while pinning object-key order, compact separators, array order, coin-array order and final newline.
> - The sidecars are diagnostic_bank.rejections.jsonl and heldout_bank.rejections.jsonl. Each line is one JSON object with exactly the fields {attempt, bank, candidate_id, reason, stratum}; attempt is the one-based whole-record or whole-instance attempt number; bank is exactly diagnostic or heldout; candidate_id is the planned input_id for a single record or pair_id for a reorder instance; stratum is the D/H stratum identifier. reason is one of MIN_COIN_ONE, GCD_DIVIDES_AMOUNT, D4_GCD_NOT_ONE, D5_GREEDY_EQUALS_MINIMUM, H2_MATCH_AMOUNT_BELOW_13, H2_ORACLE_BELOW_TWO, H4_MATCH_AMOUNT_BELOW_20, H8_ORACLE_BELOW_TWO, INT32_OVERFLOW, WITHIN_BANK_CANONICAL_COLLISION, CROSS_BANK_COLLISION or IDENTITY_PERMUTATION. For a held-out candidate that unexpectedly equals a D5 fixed key, CROSS_BANK_COLLISION is logged immediately before fatal abort, not followed by a retry. Each rejected whole-record or whole-instance attempt produces exactly one sidecar line. Sidecars use UTF-8 JSON Lines, no BOM, json.dumps(event, sort_keys=True, separators=(",",":"), ensure_ascii=False, allow_nan=False), and LF after every line including the final line.
>
> **6. Generator script freeze.** The production generator is one script with seeds hardcoded as the v1.0 literals. It accepts no command-line arguments, reads no environment variables or configuration files, and uses only the constants and procedures in this specification. Its output directory is a single fixed constant agreed in the execution protocol; it is not a run-time parameter. The script's SHA-256 is recorded and the byte-identical script is published beside the bank files before any bank generation. A diagnostic harness used for review may have a separate interface, but it is not the production generator and cannot be represented as implementing this item.
>
> **7. Interpreter pin.** Bank generation does not start until the execution protocol names the exact Python interpreter version. Generation and regeneration cross-checks use that same interpreter version and the byte-identical frozen script.
>
> **8. Cross-bank disjointness (acceptance condition and atomic publication gate).** Complete both candidate banks, collect the already-computed oracle answer once per canonical_id, serialize the two oracle-output arrays in memory, compute their file-level SHA-256 digests, add the corresponding `oracle_output_file_sha256` to every record, compute all `record_sha256` values, and serialize both bank arrays in memory. Do not open, create or write any authoritative bank file or oracle-output attachment during these steps. Then compute the full intersection of the two banks' canonical identities.
>
> The canonical-disjointness report is exactly `canonical_disjointness_report.json`. It is one UTF-8 JSON object with exactly these fields: `schema` (the literal string `lc518-canonical-disjointness-v1`), `status` (`PASS` only if the intersection is empty and every required output artifact has been written and hash-verified; otherwise `FAIL`), `diagnostic_record_count`, `diagnostic_unique_canonical_count`, `heldout_record_count`, `heldout_unique_canonical_count`, `cross_bank_collision_counts_by_stratum`, `intersection`, and `artifacts`. `intersection` is an array sorted by `canonical_id`; each element has exactly `canonical_id`, `amount`, `coins` (sorted ascending), `diagnostic_input_ids`, and `heldout_input_ids`. Each input-ID array lists every bank record carrying that canonical_id, in that bank's generation order. `cross_bank_collision_counts_by_stratum` is an object with exactly the 16 keys `D1`–`D8` and `H1`–`H8`; each value is a nonnegative integer counting sidecar rows with reason `CROSS_BANK_COLLISION` for that stratum across the two banks. The sum of these 16 values must equal the total number of `CROSS_BANK_COLLISION` rows across both sidecars. For a completed `PASS` run, H1–H8 values must all be zero. `artifacts` is an array sorted by `filename`; each element has exactly `filename` and `sha256`, where `sha256` is the lowercase SHA-256 digest of the complete file bytes. For `PASS`, `artifacts` must list exactly `diagnostic_oracle_outputs.json`, `heldout_oracle_outputs.json`, `diagnostic_bank.json`, `heldout_bank.json`, `diagnostic_bank.rejections.jsonl`, and `heldout_bank.rejections.jsonl`. For `FAIL` caused by a nonempty intersection, it lists only the two rejection sidecars. Serialize the report with `json.dumps(report, sort_keys=True, separators=(",",":"), ensure_ascii=False, allow_nan=False).encode("utf-8") + b"\n"`.
>
> **Staged publication and acceptance.** The fixed final output directory and its sibling staging directory (`OUTPUT_DIR + ".staging"`) must both be absent at preflight; otherwise abort before consuming either RNG stream. Write all candidate outputs only inside the staging directory. If the intersection is empty, write the two oracle-output attachments, the two bank files and both rejection sidecars there; flush and close each file, compute SHA-256 over its actual bytes, and verify each digest against the in-memory bytes. Only after all six files exist and verify, write the report to `canonical_disjointness_report.json.tmp`, flush and close it, then atomically rename it to `canonical_disjointness_report.json` within the staging directory. If the intersection is nonempty, write only the two rejection sidecars in staging, then write a `FAIL` report through the same temporary-file-and-atomic-rename procedure; do not write either bank file or either oracle-output attachment. In either case, after the report is the final file in staging, atomically rename the entire staging directory to `OUTPUT_DIR` on the same filesystem. The staging directory is never authoritative. An interruption or I/O error before the directory rename leaves `OUTPUT_DIR` absent and the run **NOT_ACCEPTED**; an orphan staging directory does not count as output. The final package is accepted only if `OUTPUT_DIR/canonical_disjointness_report.json` exists with `status = "PASS"`, every filename in `artifacts` exists in `OUTPUT_DIR`, and every listed SHA-256 matches the corresponding file bytes. An absent report, `FAIL` report, missing artifact, hash mismatch, or absent final directory means **NOT_ACCEPTED**. A stale report cannot survive preflight because the final and staging directories must not pre-exist.
>
> The report records every colliding canonical_id, amount, sorted denomination list and all corresponding input_ids. Under item 3d, ordinary random cross-bank collisions are rejected during diagnostic candidate generation; the final comparison is a fail-closed independent verification guard, not the primary collision-handling mechanism. It does not redraw or repair a collision that survives the earlier filters. Any nonempty final intersection is a generator/specification defect and means NOT_ACCEPTED. No seed is changed and no bank is patched. Any remedy requires an owner-reviewed version change and full regeneration from scratch under that new specification.
>
> **Justification.** This amendment replaces the known D5/H8 exemplar collision and closes the remaining fixed-record collision path by reserving every deterministic key before any random draw; it explicitly records that D5 records 3–8 can reproduce E1/E2 and must be rejected as whole records. It removes the false D4 zero-answer claim for odd coins with odd amounts, resolves the conflict between the D4 row and the whole-record redraw rule, replaces the ambiguous H1–H4 repetition failsafe with four named oracle-checked witnesses, defines all identifiers and oracle-output attachments, pins the canonical-disjointness report schema and its write gate, bounds every retry loop, requires non-identity reorder pairs, and distinguishes the diagnostic harness from the production generator. It preserves the two separate bank seed values, the bank sizes, the stratum counts, the LC518 task contract and the requirement for a full cross-bank disjointness check. No bank has been generated by GPT in preparing this draft.
