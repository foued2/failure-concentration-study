## 0. Role of this plan

This plan operationalises the six items Rev 1.4 §6.1 requires it to specify **before any fitting or inspection of held-out outcomes**: (i) the confirmatory test for \(\Delta LL\) under overlapping LOPO folds, with the resampling unit; (ii) treatment of unequal scored-set coverage across parents; (iii) the role of labelled sensitivities; (iv) fold definitions, regularization, and convergence criteria; (v) multiplicity handling across co-reported secondary summaries; and (vi) the N4-a operationalisation per Amendment E. Sections 1–4 fix data, estimand, folds, and fitting; §5 fixes the confirmatory test; §6 fixes statuses; §7 fixes multiplicity; §8 operationalises N4-a; §§9–10 fix the N2 regime-audit integration and the N3 reading with its proxy-pathway check; §11 consolidates reporting; §12 states boundaries; §13 maps compliance.

---

## 1. Data, units, eligibility

1. **Analysis rows.** The unit of the primary likelihood is the version × canonical-record row: \(Y_{v,r} \in \{0,1\}\) for mutant version \(v\) on development record \(r \in \{1,\dots,78\}\) (Rev 1.4 §6.1). The same fixed 78-record bank is used across versions; it is **development data, publicly exposed, never sealed**. Diagnostic tables from the oracle supply each version's identity, family, totals, and \(C_D(v) = M(v)/k(v)\). The oracle accounting stands as recorded in the dated erratum: 138 oracle rows (60 diagnostic + 78 held-out) covering 144 records with 6 duplicate-canonical rows. The frozen blind E3 is untouched by this plan.
2. **Primary sample.** Versions \(v\) whose full-population cell \(c(v) = (q, Z, k, \ell)\) satisfies \(B1(c) = 1\): \(k \ge 2\), \(G_c \ge 3\) distinct parents, \(D_c \ge 2\) distinct observed \(C_D\) values (Rev 1.4 §4.1, §6.1). No pooling across identities, families, or exact totals. Versions outside qualifying cells are not added to the primary estimand; any broader scoring is a separately labelled secondary analysis (Rev 1.4 §6.1).
3. **Eligibility is recomputed per dataset.** Cell support \(B1(c)\), fold-level estimability, and scored sets are recomputed from each dataset's own diagnostic tables and outcomes — the real dataset, or each §8 synthetic replication. **Within a dataset, the audited sets are fixed across permutation resamples** (§5.3 step 3, permutation-invariant); eligibility is never carried over from another dataset.

---

## 2. Predeclared estimand

The primary statistic is the equal-parent \(\Delta LL\) of Rev 1.4 §6.1, restated for self-containment:

\[
\Delta LL=\frac{1}{|\mathcal G_{\mathrm{score}}|}\sum_{g\in\mathcal G_{\mathrm{score}}}(L_{0,g}-L_{1,g}),\qquad
L_{m,g}=(|\mathcal V_g^{\mathrm{score}}|\,|\mathcal R|)^{-1}\!\!\sum_{v\in\mathcal V_g^{\mathrm{score}}}\sum_{r\in\mathcal R}\bigl[-Y_{v,r}\log\widehat\pi^{(m)}_{v,r}-(1-Y_{v,r})\log(1-\widehat\pi^{(m)}_{v,r})\bigr],
\]

with \(\mathcal R = \{1,\dots,78\}\), \(\mathcal G_{\mathrm{score}}=\{g:|\mathcal V_g^{\mathrm{score}}|>0\}\), \(m=0\) the exact-cell baseline and \(m=1\) the enriched model; positive values favour the enriched model. The population quantity targeted is the expected per-record log-likelihood gain of the enriched model over the exact-cell baseline, under the LOPO prediction rule declared in §3, averaged with **equal weight per scored parent**. Parents with no eligible scored rows are outside the estimand; their exclusion reasons are reported.

---

## 3. Folds, scored sets, and unequal coverage (Rev 1.4 item ii; fold definitions)

1. **Folds.** Exactly leave-one-parent-out (LOPO) over the parents of the primary sample. Fold \(g\): training rows = all rows of primary-sample versions whose parent \(\ne g\); test rows = parent \(g\)'s primary-sample rows. No fold variants, no repeats, no data-driven fold construction. Controls \(S0\) and versions with \(k = 0\) are outside the primary sample (\(C_D\) undefined; Rev 1.4 §7.2 \(k=0\) convention).
2. **Scored set.** For each fold, both models are scored on exactly the same test rows: the parent's versions in cells that remain present in the training fold with sufficient estimability, scored on all 78 records. Any test \(C_D\) outside its cell's training range is flagged as extrapolation and reported, never dropped after seeing its outcome (Rev 1.4 §6.1).
3. **Fold-level estimability.** Per fold and cell, report \(G_c^{\mathrm{train}}\), \(D_c^{\mathrm{train}}\), \(W_{C,c}^{\mathrm{train}}=\sum_{v\in c,\,\mathrm{train}}(C_D(v)-\overline C_{D,c}^{\mathrm{train}})^2\), separation and convergence status, and \(\sum_c W_{C,c}^{\mathrm{train}}\). A cell contributes within-cell information only when \(G_c^{\mathrm{train}}\ge2\), \(D_c^{\mathrm{train}}\ge2\), and \(W_{C,c}^{\mathrm{train}}>0\). A fold in which the enriched slope is not estimable is flagged, never treated as a null effect.
4. **Unequal scored-parent coverage.** \(|\mathcal G_{\mathrm{score}}|\) and its membership are properties of each realized dataset and can vary across resamples. Declared handling: (a) the statistic always uses each dataset's own \(\mathcal G_{\mathrm{score}}\) with equal weight per scored parent — no imputation of missing scored rows; (b) exclusion reasons per parent listed (cell absent from training, no training-fold variation, non-estimable fit); (c) reports include per-fold and per-dataset coverage tables and, under resampling, the distribution of \(|\mathcal G_{\mathrm{score}}|\) across resamples and each parent's inclusion frequency. No parent is silently added to or removed from the estimand.

## 4. Fitting, regularization, convergence (Rev 1.4 item iv)

1. **Models (Rev 1.4 §6.1, verbatim):** baseline \(\operatorname{logit}\Pr(Y_{v,r}=1)=\alpha_{c(v)}+\omega_r\); enriched \(\operatorname{logit}\Pr(Y_{v,r}=1)=\alpha_{c(v)}+\omega_r+\beta_C\,C_D(v)\). \(C_D\) enters on its **raw \((0,1]\) scale — no centering or rescaling**, so the meaning of \(\beta_C\) is fixed. \(\omega_r\) carries a **sum-to-zero (centered) constraint** over the 78 records; \(\alpha_c\) is unconstrained per exact cell. Reference/contrast coding is fixed by this sentence before any fit.
2. **Estimation.** Maximum likelihood via iteratively reweighted least squares (Fisher scoring), identical settings for both models: convergence when the relative gradient is \(\le 10^{-8}\) or after at most 200 iterations, whichever first; nonconvergence is reported.
3. **Separation rule (prespecified, deterministic given training data).** If a fit fails to converge, or \(|\widehat\beta_C| > 50\), or the Wald standard error of \(\widehat\beta_C\) exceeds \(10^{3}\), both models are **refitted with a Firth-type Jeffreys-penalty correction, applied identically to both**, and the fold is flagged "separation-handled". The rule depends only on training-fold data and is applied identically inside every fold and every resample; the count of separation-handled folds is reported per dataset and per resample. No other regularization is used; there is no tuning outside the training fold.
4. **Common slope as declared assumption.** The common \(\beta_C\) is an explicit modelling assumption (Rev 1.4 §6.1), never replaced after seeing outcomes. The **cell-specific-slope sensitivity** \(\beta_{C,c}\) is run exactly as Rev 1.4 §6.1 declares — same fold rules, same scored sets; a cell contributes a slope only when \(W_{C,c}^{\mathrm{train}}>0\); the sensitivity is reported only when at least two cells contribute; and per GPT's adjudication the report states **how many cells are slope-estimable and each estimable cell's individual support** (per-cell training version counts, \(W_{C,c}^{\mathrm{train}}\), fitted-slope stability diagnostics), never a bare pass/fail flag. Sensitivity only; divergence from the common-slope primary is reported, not resolved; no data-driven promotion.
5. **The baseline is never substituted.** No reduced baseline replaces the primary exact-cell baseline in the primary analysis (Rev 1.4 §6.1 "no pooling or silent fallback"). Any reduced-form comparison is a separately labelled secondary and is never pooled with primary results.

## 5. Confirmatory inference under overlapping LOPO folds (Rev 1.4 item i)

### 5.1 Why the naive test is excluded

LOPO training sets overlap heavily (folds \(g\) and \(g'\) differ only by the two held-out parents' rows), so per-parent scores are dependent and a paired t-test treating them as independent is not accepted (Rev 1.4 §6.1). The confirmatory calibration must therefore come from a resampling structure that preserves the dependence.

### 5.2 Null hypothesis and resampling unit (declared)

- **Null hypothesis \(H_0^{\mathrm{perm}}\):** within each eligible cell, the \(C_D\) labels are conditionally exchangeable with respect to the outcomes given the cell \((q,Z,k,\ell)\), the canonical record structure, and the parent clustering — equivalently, \(C_D\) carries **no incremental predictive content beyond the exact cell through any channel**. This is the predeclared confirmatory null corresponding to the recorded B1 target.
- **Resampling unit (v1.1, explicit):** the **within-cell \(C_D\) label, applied at the version level**. A within-cell permutation reassigns the \(C_D\) labels among the cell's versions; **each version's own 78-record outcome vector, its parent membership, its identity, and its cell membership remain attached to that version — only the \(C_D\) label moves** (equivalently, the \(M\) values are swapped within the cell, since the cell fixes \(k\) and \(\ell\)). Versions of one parent are left clustered: the permutation acts only on \(C_D\) labels inside cells; outcomes, parent assignments, records, and cell memberships are untouched. The permutation null distribution of \(\Delta LL\) therefore inherits the parent-level dependence of the outcomes automatically — no fold-independence assumption is used anywhere.
- **Status of the exactness assumption (declared; v1.1).** The exactness of the permutation null is a property of the declared null \(H_0^{\mathrm{perm}}\), **not** a consequence of holding outcome vectors intact. It holds when \(H_0^{\mathrm{perm}}\) is true of the data-generating process — in particular under N1-type nulls (\(\beta_C = 0\) with no channel coupling \(C_D\) to outcomes within cells). It **fails by construction** under N3-type processes (\(\theta_g\) couples \(C_D\) to outcomes across parents) and under N4-b-type processes; detecting exactly such violations of \(H_0^{\mathrm{perm}}\) is how the test detects incremental prediction. A rejection therefore cannot, by itself, separate the channel (§5.6); the N-control evaluations of §§8–10 characterize the rejection rate under each declared scenario so that interpretation is calibrated, and the N3 reading rule (§10, Rev 1.4 §7.2) governs the interpretation of a gain that arises through the parent-quality channel.

### 5.3 Test procedure (frozen once this plan is accepted)

1. Compute \(\Delta LL_{\mathrm{obs}}\) by the frozen pipeline of §§3–4 on the real data (both models refit in every fold).
2. **Permutation resamples.** For \(b = 1,\dots,R_{\mathrm{perm}}\): independently within each eligible cell \(c\), draw a uniform permutation of the cell's versions and reassign their \(C_D\) values accordingly (\(C_D^{(b)}(v) = C_D(\pi_c(v))\)). Because the cell fixes \(k\) and \(\ell\), this swaps \(M\) values within the cell and nothing else.
3. **Invariant target population; within-resample refitting (v1.1; replaces v1.0's "scored sets recomputed per resample").** The scored sets, fold definitions, and audited row sets are fixed by the **observed data's estimability audit** (§3) and are held **identical** across the observed statistic and every permutation resample (a permutation-invariant target population): the observed \(\Delta LL\) and every null value are computed on the same scored rows for the same folds, so the null is conditional on the same target population as the observed statistic. Within every resample, all **fitted parameters** are recomputed: both models are refit in every LOPO fold on the same training rows under the §4 rules, including the deterministic separation rule, which applies identically in the observed and permuted pipelines. No fitted quantity is carried across resamples. (Scored-set construction from changed data occurs only in the §8 synthetic replications, where each replication is its own dataset and its own audit fixes its sets.)
4. **Decision rule (v1.1; replaces v1.0's zero-centred formula).** The permutation reference distribution is generated by the same fitting procedure and **need not be centred at zero** — fitting an extra slope on a null predictor typically shifts out-of-sample \(\Delta LL\) slightly negative, so an absolute-value comparison around zero would be anti-conservative. The two-sided p-value is therefore built from the two tails of the permutation distribution itself:
\[
p_{\mathrm{R}}=\frac{1+\#\{b:\Delta LL^{(b)}\ge\Delta LL_{\mathrm{obs}}\}}{R_{\mathrm{perm}}+1},\qquad
p_{\mathrm{L}}=\frac{1+\#\{b:\Delta LL^{(b)}\le\Delta LL_{\mathrm{obs}}\}}{R_{\mathrm{perm}}+1},\qquad
p=\min\bigl(1,\;2\min(p_{\mathrm{L}},\,p_{\mathrm{R}})\bigr),
\]
with ties counted by \(\ge\) and \(\le\) respectively; reject \(H_0^{\mathrm{perm}}\) at \(\alpha = 0.05\) when \(p \le 0.05\). This is the **single confirmatory test** of the plan. The report gives \(p_{\mathrm{L}}\), \(p_{\mathrm{R}}\), \(p\), and the permutation median of \(\Delta LL\). The one-sided positive reading used by the N4-a control (§8) is \(p_{\mathrm{R}}\) with its own declared threshold; the two-sided and one-sided uses are separate predeclared procedures.

### 5.4 Exact enumeration preference

Let \(n_c\) be a cell's version count and \(n_{c,m}\) the multiplicity of each distinct \(C_D\) value in it; the number of distinct within-cell reassignments is \(n_c!/\prod_m n_{c,m}!\). If the product over eligible cells is \(\le 10^{6}\), the null distribution is computed by **exact enumeration** (no random sampling at all): this branch is preferred for its **determinism** — it has no sampling noise, whereas the Monte Carlo branch has declared resolution \(1/(R_{\mathrm{perm}}+1)\). The bound is a **declared practicality threshold, not a measured one**: its cost is at most \(10^{6}\) pipeline evaluations per dataset, and the GO-time computational plan (§8 item iii) must demonstrate that this is feasible before the enumeration branch is used; if it is not, the plan falls to the Monte Carlo branch. Otherwise Monte Carlo sampling with \(R_{\mathrm{perm}} = 9{,}999\) resamples plus the observed assignment is used, with the predeclared seed 20261114 and all seeds recorded in output. Designing this resampling structure is within the present authorization; executing it is not (§12).

### 5.5 Null granularity and the labelled secondary uncertainty summary

- The number of distinct resample statistic values is reported. When it is small, the p-value's resolution is coarse; this is stated in the report — no silent switch to a parametric or asymptotic test.
- **Labelled secondary:** a parent-level bootstrap percentile interval for \(\Delta LL\) (\(B_{\mathrm{boot}} = 2{,}000\); resampling unit = parent, with replacement; both models refit within every bootstrap resample; scored sets held to the same frozen audited sets as the observed statistic). Exploratory only; it never substitutes for the confirmatory permutation test and never supports a confirmatory claim.

### 5.6 Interpretation of a rejection (channels kept distinct)

Rejection of \(H_0^{\mathrm{perm}}\) establishes: incremental predictive information about held-out correctness beyond the exact-cell baseline, beyond the predeclared finite-sample null variability. **It does not by itself attribute a channel.** The reading — direct concentration information, latent parent-quality prediction through \(\theta_g\) (the N3 case), proxying of omitted unresolved-placement information (the N4-b case), or leakage/misspecification — is resolved only jointly with the N-control reports of §§8–10. No mechanism-level attribution from the gain alone (Rev 1.4 §7.2). In particular, under N3-structured generating processes the exchangeability null fails **by construction** (within-cell \(C_D\) proxies \(\theta_g\)); that behaviour is reported under the N3 control and its declared interpretation, not hidden and not scored as a procedure failure.

## 8. N4-a operationalisation (Rev 1.4 item vi; Amendment E sub-items (i)–(vi))

Until this plan is frozen, N4-a remains interpretive only (Rev 1.4 §7.2). Once frozen, N4-a is operationalised as follows.

**(i) Null reference procedure.** Data generated from the declared truth model (Rev 1.4 §2.5, §7.1, §7.2) under the N4-a settings: the N1 configuration — \(\Sigma\) the zero matrix (no parent effects: \(\sigma_\theta^2 = \sigma_{\theta\varphi} = \sigma_\varphi^2 = 0\), consistent with N3 being defined as N1 except \(\operatorname{Var}(\theta_g)>0\)), identity baseline shapes \(b^F_{q,s}, b^U_{q,s}\) constant across strata within each identity, \(\gamma^F = \gamma^U = 0\), \(\beta_{u\to y}=0\) — evaluated at **crossed anchors** \(t^*(q) \ne s^*(q)\) with **predeclared positive finite** \(\lambda \in \{0.5,\ 1.0,\ 2.0\}\) (weak, moderate, and strong decay \(e^{-\lambda\Delta}\) for integer-gap penalty profiles). This \(\lambda\) set is fixed by this sentence; it may be changed only by an amendment accepted through the review chain **before** any N4-a evaluation, never after results are seen. Anchor profiles, base shapes, and all generating constants are recorded before generation.

**(ii) Calibration statistic.** The calibration statistic is \(\Delta LL\) itself, computed by the frozen pipeline (§§3–5) on each generated dataset. Its finite-sample null variability is quantified by the **within-dataset permutation distribution** of §5.3–5.4 applied to that dataset (exact enumeration when the §5.4 bound holds; otherwise \(R_{\mathrm{perm}} = 9{,}999\) with recorded seeds). The same machinery that calibrates the real-data test is therefore the one audited under the null — there is no second, divergent calibration.

**(iii) Replication/resampling structure.** \(N_{\mathrm{rep}} = 100\) **independent replications** of the N4-a generating process per predeclared \(\lambda\) value. Each replication is **its own dataset**: it generates the full diagnostic tables and held-out outcomes, its estimability audit fixes its own scored sets by the §3 rules (the same audited-sets construction as §5.3 step 3, fixed once per replication), and the complete LOPO pipeline computes \(\Delta LL_{\mathrm{obs}}\) and its within-dataset permutation null on those sets. Both models are refit within every fold of every replication and within every permutation resample — the total fit count is \(N_{\mathrm{rep}}\times\bigl[(G-1)\ \text{folds} + R_{\mathrm{perm}}\times(G-1)\bigr]\times 2\) models per \(\lambda\) value, and the **computational plan demonstrating this is tractable must be stated at GO time; it is not asserted here** (the specification's finite-sum/tractability honesty carries over).

**(iv) Reproducibility criterion (v1.1: sidedness explicit).** The per-replication criterion is **one-sided positive** at per-replication level \(0.025\): a replication yields **positive exceedance** when \(\Delta LL_{\mathrm{obs}}\) exceeds the \(0.975\) quantile of its own within-dataset permutation null. One-sided positive is the declared sidedness because, under N4-a, only a **positive** gain can indicate leakage or misspecification — a negative \(\Delta LL\) cannot. This criterion is deliberately **not** the §5.3 two-sided rule (which answers the real-data confirmatory question); the two-sided and one-sided uses are separate predeclared procedures. The gain is declared **reproducible beyond the predeclared finite-sample null variability** when the exceedance count \(K\) over the \(N_{\mathrm{rep}}\) replications satisfies
\[
K \;\ge\; c_{\mathrm{crit}} \;=\; \min\bigl\{K : \Pr\bigl(\mathrm{Bin}(N_{\mathrm{rep}},\,0.025) \ge K\bigr) \le 0.05\bigr\}.
\]
With the predeclared \(N_{\mathrm{rep}} = 100\) this evaluates to \(c_{\mathrm{crit}} = 6\) (exact binomial tail \(\Pr(\mathrm{Bin}(100,0.025)\ge6) \approx 0.04 \le 0.05\), while \(\Pr(\ge5) \approx 0.11 > 0.05\)) — hand arithmetic under the §8-style illustrative exception of the specification; the exact value is confirmed by exact binomial enumeration at evaluation time under a GO, before generation, and recorded.

**(v) Evidence threshold.** \(K \ge c_{\mathrm{crit}}\) at a predeclared \(\lambda\) value constitutes, per Rev 1.4 §7.2 N4-a and Amendment D, **evidence of leakage or model misspecification under this control** — a reproducible gain beyond finite-sample null variability where the declared truth model contains no \(C_D\) pathway. \(K < c_{\mathrm{crit}}\) supports no such declaration; **an isolated positive estimate can arise by chance under a correctly specified null and is not, by itself, evidence of a data leak** (Amendment D wording, restated as binding). The empirical exceedance rate is additionally reported against the nominal \(0.025\) as a calibration diagnostic for every null scenario evaluated (N1, N2, N3, N4-a, N4-b).

**(vi) Multiplicity (v1.1: full procedure).** The N4-a family is the set of exceedance tests at the predeclared \(\lambda\) values \(\{0.5, 1.0, 2.0\}\). Per \(\lambda\), the count \(K_\lambda\) converts to an exact p-value
\[
p_\lambda \;=\; \Pr\bigl(\mathrm{Bin}(N_{\mathrm{rep}},\,0.025) \ge K_\lambda\bigr),
\]
computed exactly by binomial enumeration at evaluation time **before** any generation, from the predeclared \(N_{\mathrm{rep}} = 100\) and per-replication level \(0.025\) — no estimation, no rounding. Holm's step-down procedure is then applied at familywise \(\alpha = 0.05\): order \(p_{(1)} \le p_{(2)} \le p_{(3)}\); reject the hypothesis with \(p_{(1)}\) if \(p_{(1)} \le 0.05/3\); if and only if it was rejected, reject \(p_{(2)}\) if \(p_{(2)} \le 0.05/2\); if and only if that too was rejected, reject \(p_{(3)}\) if \(p_{(3)} \le 0.05\). A leakage/misspecification declaration names the \(\lambda\) value(s) at which rejection occurred. If \(N_{\mathrm{rep}}\) or the per-replication level is ever amended, the same formulas recompute \(c_{\mathrm{crit}}\) and the conversion **before** evaluation, through the review chain. The N4-a family is separate from the single real-data primary test and from all descriptive reports (§7).

**Real-data reading.** On real data \(\theta_g\) and \(\xi_v\) are latent; the synthetic N4-a evaluation is the **calibration audit of the declared null machinery** under crossed-affinity nulls. A real-data positive that exceeds the predeclared null quantile is "inferentially supported beyond finite-sample null variability" in the Amendment D sense; its channel attribution is still governed by §5.6 and §§9–10. Nothing is classified as leakage or model misspecification on real data except through this predeclared pathway.

## 9. N2 integration: exact per-cell regime audit

Before any N2 scoring: per eligible cell, the strong-affinity audit of Rev 1.4 §7.3 determines \(\Psi_q(k,\ell;\Delta^F,\Delta^U)\) and the regime by **exact comparisons on unrounded \(\Phi\) values** — rounded or floating-point-comparison values are never used for regime determination. Ties (\(|\Psi|>1\)) are reported as ties; regime (D) means \(M\) constant on \(\Psi\), regime (T) means \(M\) varies on \(\Psi\); the regime is determined by the audit, never by tie presence, dimensionality, or discreteness (Amendment B vocabulary, binding here). Regime-(D) cells are expected to carry zero within-cell information under N2; any persistent variation on regime-(T) cells is tie-dependent and flagged as such. The audit table (cell, \(\Psi\), tie status, regime) is reported.

## 10. N3 reading and the proxy-pathway check (Rev 1.4 §7.2, §9 item 5)

1. **Reading distinctions (binding, restated):** a positive B1 gain under N3 is reported as **consistent with latent parent-quality prediction** through the \(\theta_g\) channel; it must not be described as evidence of a direct concentration mechanism; it must not be scored as an automatic failure of the predictive target; and it is not automatically a false positive. N3 robustness is a precondition for interpreting a positive real-data B1 result.
2. **Proxy-pathway check (GPT, 2026-11-14), weighting convention now declared.** Under declared-DGP evaluation, where \(\theta_g\) is generated and therefore known: within each eligible cell with \(D_c \ge 2\), compute the version-level Pearson correlation between \(C_D(v)\) and \(\theta_{\mathrm{par}(v)}\) over that cell's scored versions. **Primary summary:** the unweighted mean of these within-cell correlations over eligible cells (equal weight per cell), with every per-cell value listed. **Labelled secondaries:** the version-weighted pooled correlation, and the parent-level variant (within each eligible cell, the correlation between parent-mean \(C_D\) and \(\theta_g\) across the cell's contributing parents). This check shows whether the intended proxy pathway actually operated, rather than merely that it was possible. On real data \(\theta_g\) is latent and the check is not computable; the §10 item 1 reading rule governs real data instead.
